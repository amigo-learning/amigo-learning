FAQ

Common questions

Is Amigo Academy an official Ministry platform?

No. Amigo Academy provides curriculum-aligned learning resources. It is not government-approved or officially accredited.

Is it really free?

Yes — core lessons, quizzes and practice papers are free to use.

Where do the videos come from?

Videos are embedded from YouTube using official embedding — nothing is downloaded or re-hosted.

Are the past papers official exam papers?

Only where clearly labelled "Official Past Paper" with a verified source. Otherwise papers are marked as practice, revision or mock materials.
