About Amigo Learning

More than a learning forum.

Amigo Learning was started on 14 August 2026 as a student-led educational initiative intended to make learning resources, study support, videos, and interactive learning opportunities easier for students across South Sudan to access.

It is designed to grow into a complete learning hub. Students can discover verified university study paths, explore video tutorials, test their understanding with quizzes, and collaborate in peer study groups.
