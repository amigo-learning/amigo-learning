Education Should Be Accessible to Everyone

Amigo Learning was created to help students access useful educational resources without financial barriers.

We provide free notes, past papers, questions and other learning resources that students can use to improve their academic performance.

Our goal is simple: help students learn, prepare for examinations and build a brighter future for South Sudan.

📢 A Message from the Founder

"Welcome to Amigo Learning! I created this learning space because I believe every student in South Sudan deserves free access to quality education.


Here you will find notes, past papers and study materials to help you succeed in your university courses.


— John Marial Chol, Founder
