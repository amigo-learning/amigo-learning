About Amigo Academy

More than a learning forum.

Amigo Academy was started on 14 August 2026 as a student-led educational initiative intended to make learning resources, study support, videos, and interactive learning opportunities easier for students across South Sudan to access.

It is designed to grow into a complete learning hub. Students can discover verified university study paths, explore video tutorials, test their understanding with quizzes, and collaborate in peer study groups.

The long-term vision is an online self-learning academy with structured lessons, student progress tracking, certificates, and personal learning dashboards.
